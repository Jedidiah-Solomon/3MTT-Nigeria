## Content gaps vs https://hordanso.net/vps-hosting-nigeria/
The marketing page is stronger in SEO and conversion messaging than the WHMCS product pages. Key gaps on product pages:

1. **Missing conversion copy blocks**
   - Marketing page includes strong value messaging (speed, security, reliability, support) while product pages are mostly transactional.
2. **Weak metadata layer on WHMCS pages**
   - Marketing page has optimized title and meta description; WHMCS product pages do not.
3. **Insufficient local-intent keyword spread**
   - Product pages underuse `Cheap VPS Hosting Nigeria` and `VPS Hosting Lagos`.
4. **Heading intent mismatch**
   - WHMCS pages use generic `Configure`/`Order Summary` headings instead of keyword-rich context blocks.
5. **Trust/benefit content absent near plan selection**
   - Add short blocks for uptime SLA, support availability, backup policy, and deployment speed near each plan CTA.
